# Generated Sales & Margin fixture

`semantic_catalog.yaml` is the governed vocabulary shared by the local analyst and the
prepared Power BI model. The remaining artifacts are reproducibly generated by
`python -m ai_powerbi_analyst.data.build fixtures/data`:

- `sales.parquet` — 48 months of deterministic client-shaped transactions.
- `driver_contributions.parquet` — Price/Volume/Mix/Discount/Cost bridge.
- `forecast_backtest.parquet` — three rolling folds for AutoETS, AutoARIMA, and both
  deterministic baselines.
- `forecast_selection.parquet` and `forecast.parquet` — explicit gate decision and the
  frozen six-month outlook produced only by the winning approved model.
- `scenario_grid.parquet` — governed price scenarios from -3% through +5%.
- `analytics.duckdb` — internal runtime mirror, opened read-only by the application.
- `manifest.json` — source-data/catalog provenance, per-file SHA-256 hashes, resolved
  runtime versions, candidate-set completeness, and the forecast approval decision.

The data is synthetic and contains no client information.
